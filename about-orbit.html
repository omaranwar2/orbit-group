The New-Age Force

Our Evolution Since 2001

Established in Malaysia in 2001, Orbit Group has evolved from a commercial and industrial security provider into a multidisciplinary organisation delivering integrated protection, intelligence, and technology solutions.

Wherever our clients operate, our purpose remains constant: to provide the intelligence, integrity, and protection they need to operate securely, respond decisively, and grow confidently.
