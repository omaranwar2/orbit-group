OUR BRAND STORY

Orbit Group is an integrated security, risk intelligence and technology group built to protect people, assets, operations, information and reputations in an increasingly complex world.

Established in Malaysia in 2001, the Group has evolved from a commercial and industrial security provider into a multidisciplinary organisation delivering integrated protection, intelligence and technology solutions.

The New-Age Force

Today, we operate through three complementary business arms:

01

Orbit Force Security

Delivers professional security personnel and specialised protection services designed to safeguard people, premises, assets and operations across diverse industries and environments.

02

Orbit Asia Risk Consultancy

Provides investigations, due diligence, intelligence and strategic risk advisory to help organisations identify threats, manage exposure and make informed decisions with confidence.

03

Orbit Global Technologies

Develops intelligent digital platforms for security, workforce and premises operations, enabling organisations to strengthen oversight, improve efficiency and operate more securely.

MORE ABOUT OUR INTEGRATED MODEL

This integrated model gives our clients a single, trusted partner across the entire risk lifecycle—from identifying threats and uncovering vulnerabilities to deploying protection on the ground and enabling real-time operational oversight through technology.

Our strength lies in the depth and diversity of our people. We bring together experienced security professionals, former law-enforcement personnel, investigators, risk consultants, forensic specialists and technology innovators, supported by disciplined operations and an unwavering commitment to confidentiality.

INTRODUCTION & OBJECTIVES

ORBIT-FORCE SECURITY SDN. BHD. is a full-fledged Commercial & Industrial Security agency incorporated in Malaysia on 6th December, 2001 and licensed by the Kementerian Keselamatan Dalam Negeri (License No. 005961 & 005962) under the Private Agency Act 1971 providing a wide range of security related services.

OUR OBJECTIVES

Providing quality security services to local and foreign industries operating in Malaysia.

As we enter a challenging new era of globalization, the importance of delivering reliable, high quality and high value service has never been more critical. At Orbit Force Security we place absolute importance in delivering the most reliable, highest value services possible to our customers.
