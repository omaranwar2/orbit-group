Comprehensive Security Services

From uniformed guarding personnel and armed rapid-response officers to specialized K9 units, mobile patrol dispatches, and intelligence threat profiling — explore Orbit Group's full operational spectrum.

Pillar 01

Loss Prevention & Guarding Services

Deploying professionally vetted and rigorously trained physical security personnel to protect critical assets, enforce site SOPs, and maintain operational continuity across high-risk environments.

Security Personnel Service

Unarmed & Static Security Personnel

Professionally trained static guards providing reliable on-ground protection, access control verification, visitor log management, perimeter patrolling, and immediate incident reporting for corporate towers, factories, and residential communities.

High-Risk Protection Service

Armed Guard Operations

Highly trained armed security personnel providing enhanced deterrent protection, rapid-response tactical readiness, and secure operational escort support for financial institutions, high-value inventory vaults, and sensitive infrastructure.

Specialised Protection Service

Canine (K9) Protection Units

Professionally trained K9 handler teams providing advanced explosives and contraband detection, night patrol deterrence, large-scale perimeter control, and immediate intruder deterrence across ports, yards, and high-security zones.

Asset Security Service

Asset & Inventory Protection

Comprehensive asset protection frameworks engineered to safeguard industrial equipment, high-value logistics cargo, sensitive supply chains, and technology assets against internal theft, vandalism, and unauthorized access.

Pillar 02

Risk Management & Security Consultancy

Helping corporate leaders identify site vulnerabilities, establish compliance benchmarks, and integrate automated monitoring systems before operational breaches occur.

Risk Management Consultancy

Threat Assessment & Site Surveys

Detailed vulnerability audits, perimeter assessments, and operational risk surveys that evaluate physical entry points, emergency egress routes, and procedural compliance gaps.

System Engineering

Customized Loss Prevention Systems

Designing integrated electronic surveillance ecosystems combining AI-driven CCTV cameras, biometric access controls, and automated visitor management software.

Mobile Security Operations

Mobile Protection & Rapid Response

GPS-tracked mobile vehicle patrols providing round-the-clock random inspection dispatches, lock-up verifications, and swift alarm escalation support.

Pillar 03

Security & Forensic Investigations

Uncovering root causes of internal loss, employee fraud, supply chain leakage, and organized theft rings through covert surveillance and forensic intelligence.

Investigation & Loss Prevention

Addressing Syndicated Losses & Internal Fraud

Intelligence-led forensic investigations targeting organized warehouse shrinkages, corporate procurement fraud, employee cartels, and covert intellectual property theft, executed with strict non-disclosure secrecy.

Confidential Integrity Protocols

Fact-Based Audit Evidence

Our corporate investigators provide comprehensive, legally admissible evidence reports complete with digital chain-of-custody documentation, helping leadership take decisive legal and disciplinary action.

Threat Intelligence & Profiling

Threat Detection & Early Warning

Continuous threat monitoring across regional supply routes, facility surroundings, and corporate operational nodes to detect political, environmental, or social unrest early.

Personality Profiling & Background Screening
