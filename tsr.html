Trucking Security Requirements (TSR)

Professional training based on TAPA APAC Trucking Security Requirements (TSR) to help transport operators, fleet managers, and logistics providers safeguard high-value goods in transit, mitigate hijacking risks, and achieve audit readiness.

In-Transit Protection Benchmark

Understanding TAPA TSR Standards

The TAPA Trucking Security Requirements (TSR) establish industry-recognised operational security benchmarks for road freight operations. They apply to hard-sided vehicles, rigid body trucks, box trailers, fixed-body vans, and sea containers in transit.

Implementing TSR standards reduces exposure to cargo theft during transit, improves route control, enhances driver safety awareness, and aligns road transport operations with global customer expectations.

TSR Certification Tiers Overview

Level 1 (Highest Security) Hard-sided trucks and trailers equipped with electronic high-security locking systems, real-time GPS tracking, panic buttons, and 24/7 monitoring room integration.
Level 2 (Medium Security) Standard hard-sided or fixed-body commercial transport assets incorporating tamper-evident seals, scheduled check-ins, and mandatory secure parking protocols.
Level 3 (Basic Security) Baseline physical and procedural controls for regional feed fleets and third-party logistics partners, establishing foundational driver vetting and route compliance.

Curriculum Highlights

TSR Training Pillars & Modules

In-depth training modules covering physical, technical, and procedural transport security controls.

01 Cargo Protection

Cargo Protection & Compartment Security

Practical approaches for protecting high-value cargo during transit, including ISO 17712 high-security seal management, tamper-evident locks, and hard-sided vehicle compartment safeguards.

02 Driver Security

Driver Security Awareness & Vetting

Improving driver threat awareness, pre-employment background screening protocols, anti-hijacking evasive behavior, communication dispatches, and personal safety responsibilities on the road.

03 Route Security

Route Planning & Journey Risk Assessment

Evaluating journey risks along designated transport corridors, establishing mandatory secure parking locations, defining geo-fenced routes, and managing unauthorized stoppage protocols.

04 Incident Response

Incident Response & Emergency Escalation

Developing swift response protocols for hijacked vehicles, stolen cargo, unauthorized door openings, route deviations, and emergency coordination with local law enforcement.

05 Fleet Controls

Fleet Security Controls & Telematics

Integration of GPS tracking telematics, remote engine immobilizers, driver duress panic buttons, real-time control room monitoring, and automated door-sensor alerts.

06 Audit Readiness

TSR Audit & Assessment Readiness

Preparing internal teams to conduct gap assessments, maintain transport logs, verify subcontractor compliance, and achieve formal TAPA TSR certification.
