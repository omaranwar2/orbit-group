Industries We Serve

Integrated solutions for high-risk, high-value environments.

Orbit Group supports organisations across corporate, industrial, logistics, retail, public sector and critical infrastructure environments through an integrated combination of security operations, risk consultancy, intelligence-led protection and smart technology solutions.

01

Corporate & Commercial

Office towers, headquarters, business parks and commercial buildings requiring access control, visitor management and professional on-site protection.

02

Manufacturing & Industrial

Factories, plants and production facilities with workforce movement, asset protection and operational security needs.

Logistics & Supply Chain
