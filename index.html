ABOUT ORBIT FORCE

Integrated Security & Risk Management for Malaysia’s Leading Organizations

Since 2001, Orbit Force has delivered comprehensive security solutions to government agencies, multinationals, and leading Malaysian enterprises. From physical security to boardroom advisory, we provide the full spectrum of protection.

To be the leading security and risk management service provider in the region, excelling in quality management, human resource development, customer service, and innovative security solutions.

OUR MISSION

To provide quality, trained, and effective integrated security solutions based on customer requirements, positioning ourselves as Malaysia’s most trusted security and risk management partner.

OUR BRAND STORY

Orbit Force was established on December 6, 2001 as Orbit-Force Security Sdn. Bhd., licensed under the Ministry of Home Affairs (Private Agency Act 1971). Over the past two decades, we have grown from a security services provider into Malaysia’s integrated security and risk management organization.

THE NEW-AGE FORCE

Today, we operate through two divisions:

Orbit Force Security Services

Provides manned guarding, mobile patrols, security technology solutions, and training. With 1,000+ trained personnel across 8 locations, we deliver reliable, 24/7 security services nationwide.

Orbit Group Partners

Delivers law enforcement intelligence and forensic expertise to support corporate needs. Our team of former senior police officers, fraud examiners, and forensic accountants handles fraud investigations, risk advisory, and executive protection.
