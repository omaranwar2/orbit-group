Our Core Practice Areas

Risk Consulting Pillars

Integrated advisory services tailored to safeguard corporate integrity, supply chain continuity, and organizational assets.

Advisory Domain 01

Risk Management & Consultancy

Comprehensive threat assessments, physical site surveys, loss prevention audits, and customized defense architecture designed to resolve site-specific operational vulnerabilities.

Assisting organizations in achieving compliance with TAPA (Transport Asset Protection Association) and Responsible Business Alliance (RBA) global standards.

Advisory Domain 02

Security Investigations & Forensic Audits

Confidential corporate investigations addressing internal theft, supply chain leaks, procurement fraud, organized retail crime, and employee misconduct via forensic intelligence.

Conducted under strict non-disclosure protocols with legally admissible chain-of-custody documentation to protect executive decision-making.

Advisory Domain 03

Threat Intelligence & Risk Profiling

Proactive threat profiling, regional supply route threat tracking, executive background screenings, and early warning systems designed to mitigate geopolitical and operational exposure.

Provides decision-makers with structured intelligence feeds to safeguard key personnel, trade secrets, and corporate reputations.

Advisory Domain 04

Certified Training Programs (FSR & TSR)

Specialised training courses covering Facility Security Requirements (FSR) and Trucking Security Requirements (TSR) designed for warehouse managers, logistics controllers, and security officers.

Prepares internal teams to build, maintain, and audit international supply chain security benchmarks.
